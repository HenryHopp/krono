Privacy

Short version: Krono keeps nothing. Everything you type stays in your own browser, on your own device.

What Krono stores

Your shifts, pay settings, budget items and savings goal are saved in your browser's local storage. That storage belongs to your device and your browser only.

There is no account, no login, no database and no server behind Krono. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, and the author of this app cannot see any of it.

Cookies and tracking

Krono sets no cookies, loads no third-party scripts, embeds no fonts or libraries from other sites, and runs no analytics. There is nothing here that follows you.

What the host can see

The page itself is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub records ordinary request information such as your IP address and browser type when the page is downloaded. That is outside Krono's control and is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. It concerns the page request only — never the contents of your shifts or pay.

Backup files

"Save backup file" writes a file to your own device. Where that file then goes — a folder, a cloud drive, another phone — is entirely your choice. Krono never uploads it.

Deleting your data

Clearing your browser's site data for this address erases everything Krono holds, permanently and immediately. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Children

Krono is a personal calculator with no data collection, so it poses no privacy risk to users of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date below changes with it. Since nothing is collected, changes are unlikely to affect you.

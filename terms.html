Terms

Krono is a personal tool, offered for free and as is.

The figures are estimates

Krono calculates what your pay is likely to be from the shifts and rates you enter. It is a helper, not a payroll system.

Your real payslip is the only authoritative document. Always check it. If Krono and your payslip disagree, your payslip wins — and it is worth finding out why they differ.

Krono cannot know about changes to your contract, your tax card, collective agreement rates, holiday pay, corrections or anything else your employer applies. Keeping the settings current is up to you.

Not professional advice

Nothing here is payroll, tax, legal or financial advice. For questions about your actual pay, speak to your employer, your union or a qualified adviser.

Not affiliated with anyone

Krono is an independent personal project. It is not made by, endorsed by, or connected to any employer, payroll provider or scheduling service.

No warranty

The app is provided without warranty of any kind. It may contain errors, and it may become unavailable at any time. You use it at your own risk, and the author accepts no liability for decisions made on the basis of its figures.

Your data is your responsibility

Everything lives in your browser. Clearing your browser data, losing your device or switching phones will lose it unless you have saved a backup file. Save one now and then.
